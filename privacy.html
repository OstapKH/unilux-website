Privacy notice

About this notice

Updated 19 September 2026. UniLux is a schedule companion, independently operated and not an official university application. Publisher: OkSolutions. Privacy contact: support-unilux@gmail.com

What the app accesses

After you choose Connect, the app opens university and Microsoft sign-in pages. It requests your enrolled courses, timetable, teachers, rooms and buildings. It saves the timetable, last-fetch time, course visibility choices and reminder preference on your device. The app does not read or store your password itself. The embedded browser can retain sign-in cookies, subject to university expiry and device settings.

Why data is used

These data let you view your timetable offline, choose visible courses and optionally receive class reminders. After your first connection, the native app tries to refresh using the existing university session whenever you launch it or return from the background. There is no scheduled background polling. If the session is unavailable or the connection fails, the app keeps the saved timetable and last-update time and offers a manual Refresh button. Automatic attempts do not open sign-in screens. Providing university access is necessary for your personal timetable; sample mode needs no university sign-in.

Where data goes

Sign-in and timetable requests go directly to university and Microsoft services over HTTPS. They receive ordinary connection information such as your IP address and information you enter on their pages. No UniLux backend, advertising or analytics service is configured. Local reminders are scheduled on your device; UniLux does not register a remote push token. Store/platform services and your chosen backups operate under their own policies.

Retention and protection

The last successfully fetched timetable stays on your device until replaced or removed. Session cookies follow the university’s expiry rules or can be cleared with Forget sign-in & remove data in an installed build. Schedule storage is protected by the app sandbox but has no additional application-layer encryption. Android backup is disabled; iOS backups may retain copies. Lock-screen notification previews may reveal class details. Protect your device and adjust notification previews in its settings.

Your controls and rights

You can turn reminders off, remove the cached timetable and reminders, or clear the remembered sign-in cookies and saved timetable. These actions do not delete your university account, enrollment or server records. They do not sign you out of Safari or other apps. Cookie removal is unavailable in Expo Go. You may have rights of access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, depending on the processing and applicable law. University record corrections should be requested from the university. You can complain to Luxembourg’s CNPD or another competent supervisory authority.

Updates

This notice is updated when the app’s data practices change. There are no automated decisions about admission, grades or eligibility in UniLux.
