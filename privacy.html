Privacy Policy

About this notice

Updated 24 September 2026. UniLux is a schedule companion, independently operated and not an official university application. Publisher: OkSolutions. Privacy contact: support-unilux@gmail.com. Public policy: https://ostapkh.github.io/unilux-website/privacy.html

What the app accesses

After you choose Connect, the app opens university and Microsoft sign-in pages. It requests your enrolled courses, timetable, teachers, rooms and buildings. It saves the timetable, last-fetch time, course visibility choices and reminder preference on your device. The app does not read or store your password itself. The embedded browser can retain sign-in cookies, subject to university expiry and device settings.

Why data is used

These data let you view your timetable offline, choose visible courses and optionally receive class reminders. After your first connection, the native app tries to refresh using the existing university session whenever you launch it or return from the background. There is no scheduled background polling. If the session is unavailable or the connection fails, the app keeps the saved timetable and last-update time and offers a manual Refresh button. Automatic attempts do not open sign-in screens. Providing university access is necessary for your personal timetable; sample mode needs no university sign-in.

Where data goes

Sign-in and timetable requests go directly to university and Microsoft services over HTTPS. They receive ordinary connection information such as your IP address and information you enter on their pages. No UniLux backend, advertising or analytics service is configured. Local reminders are scheduled on your device; UniLux does not register a remote push token. Store/platform services and your chosen backups operate under their own policies.

Retention and protection

The last successfully fetched timetable stays on your device until replaced or removed. Session cookies follow the university’s expiry rules or can be cleared with Forget sign-in & remove data in an installed build. Schedule storage is protected by the app sandbox but has no additional application-layer encryption. Android backup is disabled; iOS backups may retain copies. Lock-screen notification previews may reveal class details. Protect your device and adjust notification previews in its settings.

Remembered sessions and access risks

The embedded browser may retain university and Microsoft authentication cookies on your device. UniLux uses that browser session to request your schedule without asking you to sign in again while the portal accepts it. There is no promised session duration. Browser cookie retention and server-side validity are different: keeping a cookie does not guarantee access. Expiry, revocation, password changes, MFA or university policy can require a new sign-in. UniLux does not need you to send cookies or tokens to the publisher. Anyone able to use your unlocked device, or malware able to compromise it, may obtain access to your saved timetable or an active portal session. A portal session can authorize more university functions than the timetable data UniLux requests.

Device, backup and notification exposure

Course choices, teacher names, room locations and class times can reveal your studies, routine and likely whereabouts. Someone with access to your device, backups, screenshots or notification previews may see this information. App sandboxing and HTTPS reduce some risks but cannot guarantee security on a lost, shared, rooted, jailbroken or otherwise compromised device. iOS backups and platform/device-transfer features may retain data beyond its removal from the app. UniLux cannot delete copies held in backups or screenshots. Use a device passcode, keep your system updated and hide notification previews if needed. Removing the timetable alone does not remove remembered sign-in cookies.

University services and connection records

Each automatic or manual refresh contacts university services and can create access logs associated with your account, IP address, device/browser information and request times. Authentication may also contact Microsoft. These providers control their own logs, cookies, retention, security checks and international processing; this notice does not replace their privacy notices. A privacy notice or your choice to connect is not university approval of this integration. Automatic access may be challenged, limited or blocked, and account restrictions may apply under the university's rules. Such a restriction is a possibility, not a confirmed consequence of using UniLux.

Support and public policy website

If you email support-unilux@gmail.com, the publisher receives your email address and whatever message or attachments you send; Google's email service processes that correspondence. Do not send passwords, MFA codes, session cookies, sign-in URLs or unredacted timetable screenshots. Support correspondence is separate from the on-device timetable and is not erased by removing app data; contact the privacy address to request its deletion, subject to applicable obligations. The public policy and support pages are hosted on GitHub Pages, whose operator receives connection information when you visit. App-store, operating-system, email, hosting and backup providers may process information outside your country under their own arrangements. No complete review of those arrangements is implied by this notice.

Your controls and rights

You can turn reminders off, remove the cached timetable and reminders, or clear the remembered sign-in cookies and saved timetable. These actions do not delete your university account, enrollment or server records. They do not sign you out of Safari or other apps. Cookie removal is unavailable in Expo Go. You may have rights of access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, depending on the processing and applicable law. University record corrections should be requested from the university. You can complain to Luxembourg’s CNPD or another competent supervisory authority.

Updates

This notice is updated when the app’s data practices change. There are no automated decisions about admission, grades or eligibility in UniLux.
