Sign in and refresh

Choose Connect for your first timetable download. After that, UniLux tries to update on each launch and return to the app. If your session expires, tap Refresh at the top of the screen to sign in again. A failed refresh keeps the last successfully saved schedule.

Remove data

In Settings, Remove saved timetable & reminders deletes the local timetable and scheduled reminders. Forget sign-in & remove data also clears the app’s sign-in cookies in installed native builds. It does not revoke your university account or clear Safari.

Report a problem

Before sending a report, remove passwords, authentication codes, full sign-in links and personal timetable details from screenshots. Include your app version, phone model, OS version and the steps that failed.

Accessibility

Buttons and navigation expose accessibility labels. If you encounter an accessibility issue, describe the screen, assistive technology and expected behavior when contacting the publisher.
