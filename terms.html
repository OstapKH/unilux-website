Usage information

Purpose

UniLux displays a personal copy of your schedule. Use an account you are authorized to access. It does not enroll you in courses, modify university records, or replace the university portal.

Schedule accuracy

The saved timetable may be out of date. Check the last-fetch time and refresh when needed. Confirm important class changes, cancellations and examinations with the official portal. Reminder delivery depends on device permissions and operating-system scheduling.

University sign-in

The university controls account access, session expiry and authentication requirements. The app cannot guarantee uninterrupted access or compatibility with future portal changes. University/Microsoft terms govern their own services.

Time zone

Dates and times use Luxembourg local time, including summer daylight-saving changes. The schedule label uses Europe/CET (Luxembourg); calculations use the Europe/Luxembourg time zone.

Independent application

No affiliation or university endorsement is claimed. Your statutory rights remain unaffected.
