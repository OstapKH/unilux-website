After connection, the native app attempts to fetch your timetable when launched or resumed from the background using its remembered browser session. If authentication is required, automatic fetching stops and you can choose Refresh to sign in. Do not export or share university session cookies or authentication tokens. To remove remembered access, use Forget sign-in & remove data; removing only the saved timetable is not a sign-out.
